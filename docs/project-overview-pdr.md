# MechvibesDX — Project Overview & Product Definition Report (PDR)

## 1. Product Statement & Core Mission
**MechvibesDX** is a cross-platform desktop application designed to provide customizable audio feedback for keyboard keystrokes, mouse clicks, ambient environmental audio, and streaming lo-fi background music.

Designed as an upgraded successor to classic soundboard tools (such as Mechvibes v1), MechvibesDX delivers zero-latency sound output, a rich Dioxus GUI, built-in catalog downloads, and custom soundpack loading.

## 2. Key Target Audience & Use Cases
- **Mechanical Keyboard Enthusiasts**: Simulating mechanical switch feel and sound profiles (e.g., Cherry MX Blue, Holy Panda, Topre, Cream switches) on laptop or membrane keyboards.
- **Content Creators & Broadcasters**: Adding rich tactile audio feedback during live streams, coding streams, or video demonstrations.
- **Accessibility & Focus**: Providing clear auditory feedback for visually impaired users or focus-enhancing lo-fi music for productivity.
- **Education & Gaming**: Audio feedback for touch-typing training or retro gaming sound effects.

## 3. Product Scope & Core Features
- **Keystroke & Mouse Click Playback**: Immediate low-latency sound triggers on keypress and keyrelease.
- **Global Hotkey Toggle**: `Ctrl+Alt+M` for instant global mute/unmute.
- **In-App Catalog & Remote Installer**: Direct browsing and automated ZIP extraction of soundpacks into the local app directory.
- **Legacy V1 Converter**: Automated conversion of legacy Mechvibes `config.json` structures into MechvibesDX V2 format.
- **Background Ambiance & Lo-Fi Music**: Built-in ambiance and background audio streams with independent volume controls.
- **System Tray Service**: Continuous background operation with right-click tray context menus.
